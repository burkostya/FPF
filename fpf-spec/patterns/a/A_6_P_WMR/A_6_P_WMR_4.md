### A.6.P.WMR:4 - Solution

**Normative method boundary.** The Conformance Checklist states each enforceable method duty once and assigns it to an applying practitioner, author/modeler, or accountable subject owner. Sections 4.0-4.8 explain the route, drafting shapes, and model-side constraints; they do not add parallel duties. Ontic identity, obtaining, polarity, non-entailment, and admissibility remain declarative.

**Stable WMR lens.** Treat the boundary wording as one use-specific relation-bearing claim over an exact entity, exact related object, direct governor, and four orthogonal claim dimensions—not as a participant kind, relation kind, or universal input/result family.

The method handles one relation-bearing claim at a time. The trigger word stays in view only until the exact entity, related object, relation, orthogonal claim dimensions, and governor are recoverable. The result then replaces the compressed phrase with the shortest ordinary direct sentence and continues under the governing pattern.

#### A.6.P.WMR:4.0 - Thin recovery core and conditional interfaces

The stable WMR core is exactly one `A.6.P`-isolated relation-bearing claim, one exact entity and related object, four orthogonally recovered claim dimensions, exactly one of four truthful exit families, and one ordinary sentence. The fourth family is an exact non-assertability result whose reason is independently `factually unsupported`, `missing-information`, or `missing-governor`; only `missing-governor` is an ontology blocker that names the affected receiving use and future owner. WMR owns that recovery and stop. It does not absorb the algorithms, checklists, or ontics of the patterns to which an exit leads.

| Conditional interface | Minimum condition for opening it | Direct return consumed here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| direct subject pattern or `A.6.1` | the exact relation or one declared operation application is already the receiver's current question | one readable direct subject-relation claim (positive obtaining or governed negative), one exact declaration-local application binding, or its exact non-assertability result |
| `A.6.RCD` | no direct relation closes the named receiving use, and a substrate-admitted compound claim, repeated predicate semantics, or relation-kind question is current | that owner's lightest direct claim, local compound claim, reusable-definition or conditional kind-admission continuation, or exact blocker; WMR does not reproduce the derivation or disposition algorithm |
| `A.15.PROD` | production-work participation, entity-identity inception, or production completion is explicitly the current receiving question | one local production claim or that branch's exact blocker; WMR does not reproduce the branch basis |
| `A.15.1`, then `F.18` when naming is needed | an action nominal or plan-like label is being relied on as one performed occurrence | one exact Work occurrence admitted under `U.Work` at the required granularity or the exact lowered neighboring object/blocker; durable naming opens only after that result |
| `A.3.4` and the direct transformation-composition owner | the current relation-bearing claim actually depends on an actual change or positive transformation composition | one independently identified transformation, one governed composition result, or the exact missing-governor/missing-substrate blocker |
| evidence, assurance, naming, delivery, acceptance, transfer, publication, or another subject owner | the receiving use additionally needs that distinct claim | only that owner's exact claim, judgment, name, occurrence, or blocker; none becomes a WMR field or makes the recovered relation obtain |

The substantial interfaces retained below are necessary only where they distinguish which exit the practitioner chooses or demonstrate that two tempting exits produce different results. Repetition of a neighbor's full basis is not evidence of correctness and is removed wherever WMR can consume the neighbor's direct return instead.

#### A.6.P.WMR:4.1 - Five recovery questions

| Question | Recovered answer |
| --- | --- |
| 1. What exact entity is this? | One exact referent under its own admitted kind. Classification as an `Input`, `Output`, `Result`, `Outcome`, `Deliverable`, or `Handoff` kind does not follow from the boundary word. |
| 2. Relative to which exact object is it being named? | One exact `U.MethodDescription`, intended work, dated work, transformation, evaluation work, commitment, delivery occurrence, acceptance work, transfer occurrence, receiving work, or another directly governed object. Several current objects require separate relation-bearing claims. |
| 3. Which exact relation gives the word its meaning here? | One direct relation or relation-bearing claim, its condition, and its governing pattern. Similar timing, co-location, a shared label, a plan row, or a diagram edge is insufficient. |
| 4. What are the claim dimensions? | The claim subject (reusable declaration, particular intended relation, commitment relation, or particular subject relation), modality and temporal extent (generic, intended/planned, actually obtaining now, or historically obtained at one exact extent, with commitment fulfilment separate), polarity, and recovery/support state (governed-and-assertable, factually unsupported, missing-information, or missing-governor) are recovered separately. Any current direct participant semantics or declaration-local operation binding is named as such. |
| 5. What is the shortest ordinary sentence, and can the engineer stop? | The result is one sentence stating only the direct claim or exact non-assertability result. Occurrence identity, provenance, evidence, assurance, publication, representation, or another separately governed object opens only when the named receiving use also needs it. |

A synonym does not answer question 3. `Used as input`, `produced as output`, `gave the result`, and `was handed off` remain compressed until the obtaining relation and its participants are recoverable.

#### A.6.P.WMR:4.2 - Orthogonal claim dimensions before actuality

The current receiving use records all four dimensions separately. They constrain one another but do not form one mutually exclusive posture list.

| Claim dimension | Recovered answer | Non-inference guard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claim subject | One reusable declaration, particular intended relation, commitment relation, or particular subject relation. | Selecting the subject decides neither whether it obtains nor its polarity, time, or support. |
| Modality and temporal extent | `generic`; `intended/planned`; `actually obtaining` at the current exact extent; or `historically obtained` at one exact past extent. For a commitment relation, state fulfilment of the promised relation separately. | An obtaining commitment does not make the promised relation fulfilled; a relation that obtained in the past does not become unresolved merely because it is not current. |
| Polarity | Positive or negative under one exact predicate, condition, and governor. | A governed negative claim individuates no obtaining relation occurrence. Absent support or missing information does not establish negative polarity. |
| Recovery/support state | `governed-and-assertable`, `factually unsupported`, `missing-information`, or `missing-governor`. | This state reports whether the selected claim can be asserted; it neither makes nor prevents the subject relation from obtaining. |

**Well-formedness constraint `WMR-WF1` — orthogonal claim dimensions.** For one relation-bearing claim, claim subject, modality and exact temporal extent, polarity, and recovery/support state are separately recoverable. None substitutes for another: an obtaining commitment does not fulfil its promised relation; a historically obtained positive relation remains positive at its governed extent; a governed negative claim individuates no obtaining relation occurrence; and factually unsupported, missing-information, or missing-governor supplies neither polarity nor obtaining.

`Factually unsupported` applies when an applicable governor is known but the available facts fail to support the proposed assertion; no opposite polarity follows without its own basis. `Missing-information` applies when the governor is known but one named fact or information basis needed for the answer is unavailable. `Missing-governor` applies when the exact participants and question are known but no current direct predicate, condition, or owner closes it.

A current commitment is expressible without collapsing fulfilment only when its exact commitment `RelationKind`, participant meanings, extent, obtaining predicate, and direct owner are named; a local id such as `COM-17` is not that settlement. The promised delivery remains intended and unfulfilled until its own exact token, owner, and facts establish fulfilment. A separately stated past case fact remains positive at its governed extent after its named participants and exact past extent satisfy the governor's obtaining predicate: `CF-17 was consumed as a resource by W-204 during I-204 under RelationKind MachiningWorkConsumesResource from MFG-WORK-REL-2026`; neither the published predicate nor the assertion that records it supplies that historical fact, and it need not obtain now to remain historically true.

A change in any dimension is substantive. A plan does not become an actually obtaining relation because its date arrives; a later observation does not retroactively manufacture a missing obtaining relation; and a stronger support state does not substitute for the subject relation's own obtaining facts.

#### A.6.P.WMR:4.3 - Four truthful exits

The exit is selected only after the four claim dimensions have been recovered. It determines the next action; it is not a fifth claim classification.

| Exit | Use it when | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact direct subject relation claim | The exact direct governor supplies the `RelationKind` token, participant meanings, predicate, applicability, and accountable owner. For a positive claim, a separately stated case fact says that the exact named participants at the exact extent satisfy the obtaining predicate. For a negative claim, the direct owner additionally supplies an explicit applicable negative or non-obtaining criterion or closure basis, and separately stated case facts satisfy that basis at the exact extent; failure or unavailability of the positive fact is not such a basis. | The result is the shortest positive or negative direct sentence with the token and resolving owner. Any positive obtaining occurrence, assertion episteme, local id, and evidence item remain distinct; a governed negative claim individuates no obtaining occurrence. |
| Exact `A.6.1` operation-application binding | One particular declared operation application and exact bound value satisfy the declaration-local argument or result binding predicate, extent, kind, cardinality, and identity rule. | The result states only that exact binding. It neither identifies dated work nor says the work produced, first constituted, delivered, or accepted the bound entity. |
| Local `A.15.PROD` or `A.6.RCD` claim | The receiving question is one local production-work, entity-identity-inception, or production-completion claim, or another local compound relation-bearing claim selected under `A.6.RCD` disposition 2 whose predicate is admitted by the selected substrate and for which no new occurrence kind is needed. | The result is the local claim under its exact base governors and the lightest sufficient `A.6.RCD` disposition. |
| Exact non-assertability result | The governor is known but the separately required fact fails (`factually unsupported`) or is unavailable (`missing-information`), or no current direct relation, truthful application binding, or local disposition-2 derivation under an admitted substrate closes the exact participants and receiving use (`missing-governor`). | The result names the selected polarity and extent, then the failed fact condition, missing fact or information, or missing governor. Only a `missing-governor` result additionally names the affected receiving use and future owner. It asserts neither a positive fallback relation nor the opposite polarity. |

A case-local positive assumption is admissible only when two premises are stated independently. The first points to an already published project relation-specification edition and names the exact `RelationKind` token, participant meanings, obtaining predicate, applicability, and accountable owner. The second states as a didactic world-side fact—not as a token, specification, work or transformation identity, chronology, record, assertion, or evidence item—that the exact participants at the exact extent satisfy that predicate. A positive sentence requires both. If only the first is present and the fact fails or is unavailable, return `factually unsupported` or `missing-information`; reserve `missing-governor` for absence of the first premise. WMR neither publishes the case-local token nor copies its relation specification.

A case-local governed negative sentence requires analogous independence: a current direct governor must make the negative or non-obtaining criterion or closure basis explicit for the exact participants and extent, and separate case facts must satisfy that basis. Merely failing to support the positive predicate, lacking its required fact, finding no record, or observing no event is not a negative premise. The negative sentence states the governed non-obtaining claim and individuates no relation occurrence.

The rejected `MethodDescriptionSlotFillingInWorkRelation` is not a fallback. A method-description field, planned filling, compatible type, stored reference, matching token, work-card row, or nearby result record establishes no actual participant relation by itself.

#### A.6.P.WMR:4.4 - Ordinary sentence shapes

These shapes are informative drafting aids. A practitioner **MAY** use one after the checklist result is known. Only dimensions current for the receiving use appear. In either direct-relation shape, the relation wording is an ordinary reading of one exact `RelationKind` token with its resolving direct pattern or relation-specification edition; any assertion id remains outside that governor position. The actually-obtaining and historically-obtained shapes are available only after a separately stated fact says that their exact participants and extent satisfy the named predicate; the compact sentence need not copy the fact fixture or an evidence package.

```text
Reusable declaration:
  Under <applicability>, <exact declaration> declares that <exact entity position>
  has <participant or predicate meaning>; no particular intended or obtaining relation follows.

Particular intended relation:
  <exact WorkPlan or other intended-work episteme> states that <exact entity>
  is intended to <direct relation> <exact related object> under <condition>;
  actual obtaining remains open.

Actually obtaining now:
  <exact entity> <direct relation in ordinary words> <exact related object>
  during <current exact extent>; governed by <RelationKind token> under <direct pattern or relation-specification edition>.

Historically obtained:
  <exact entity> <direct relation in ordinary words> <exact related object>
  during <exact past extent>; governed by <RelationKind token> under <direct pattern or relation-specification edition>.

Governed negative:
  During <exact extent>, <exact entity> did not <direct relation in ordinary words> <exact related object>
  under <RelationKind token and direct owner>; <separate case facts> satisfy
  <the owner's explicit negative or non-obtaining criterion or closure basis>,
  and no relation occurrence is individuated.

Obtaining commitment, promised relation separate:
  <accountable subject> is committed to <promised relation> for <promisee>
  during <commitment extent>; the promised relation remains <intended | unfulfilled | fulfilled at exact extent>
  under its own <RelationKind token and direct owner>.

Factually unsupported:
  The <positive or negative> claim that <exact relation sentence> is not assertable
  because the available facts fail <named condition>; no opposite polarity follows.

Missing information:
  Whether <exact entity> <candidate direct relation> <exact related object> obtains
  is unresolved because <named required fact or information basis> is unavailable.

Missing governor:
  Whether <exact entity> <candidate direct relation> <exact related object> obtains
  is unresolved because <exact governor> is absent;
  the unresolved question names <accountable subject-pattern owner> as the future owner
  for supply, rejection, or reframing of that relation for <receiving use>.
```

For example, `CF-17 was not consumed by W-204` does not follow merely because the positive consumption fact fails or is unavailable. It closes as a governed negative sentence only if the machining-work owner supplies an applicable non-consumption criterion or complete closure basis for the exact quantity, work, and extent and separate case facts satisfy it. Otherwise the proposed positive claim remains `factually unsupported` or `missing-information`, or the relation question remains `missing-governor`, according to the independently recovered deficit.

A practitioner **MAY** retain the trigger word as optional Plain orientation after the direct sentence is recoverable. The direct sentence and its dimensions, not the familiar label or support state, carry the claim into planning, work, evaluation, delivery, acceptance, transfer, or receiving use.

#### A.6.P.WMR:4.5 - Composition and universal-result stop

**Invariant `WMR-I1` — ontology economy.** No universal work-result, transformation-result, production, input, output, outcome, deliverable, handoff, evidence, actual-filling, or status relation or kind follows from boundary-word recovery. **Invariant `WMR-I2` — transformation non-entailment.** No actual transformation follows from a method, plan, desired state, model, description, evaluation result, publication, transfer, flow arrow, adjacency, shared work, or common affected referent.

When the repaired claim depends on transformation composition, its exact participants and receiving question pass to `A.3.4` and the current direct composition owner. WMR consumes only their return: an independently retained set of transformations plus either one governed composition claim or the exact missing-governor or missing-substrate blocker. It does not restate or evaluate their contribution, compatibility, composition, or reidentification algorithm.

The blocker reaches only the composition-dependent claim; independent work, change, production, evaluation, delivery, acceptance, transfer, and receiving-use questions continue under their own governors.

#### A.6.P.WMR:4.6 - Recognition and assurance remain separate

For ordinary recognition, the five questions and one of four truthful exit families are enough. A practitioner **MAY** open a separate assurance branch when a receiving use additionally needs evidence, warrant, assurance, gate, currentness, publication, or reliance. That branch receives the exact direct subject claim, exact `A.6.1` application binding, exact local `A.15.PROD`/`A.6.RCD` claim, or exact non-assertability result together with its direct-governor reference. A non-assertability result independently distinguishes `factually unsupported`, `missing-information`, and `missing-governor`; only the last is an ontology blocker that names the affected use and future owner. Support or assurance neither changes the claim dimensions nor makes the subject relation obtain.

DPF or FPF authoring may trigger the applicable E.19, A.10, B.3, or other assurance checks. Those checks remain with their owners rather than becoming a second WMR checklist.

#### A.6.P.WMR:4.7 - Boundary-word restoration through related object and direct relation

This group is an informative recognition palette, not a taxonomy. A row suggests the likely relative object and direct relation; the result then leaves the palette for the direct governing pattern. Several rows can apply to the same entity at different times or for different receiving uses, and they remain separate claims. Every relation description in the table is candidate semantics only: terms such as affected-referent, resource-use, premise-use, transfer, or delivery are not themselves `RelationKind` tokens or positive governors.

| Encountered wording | Recovery direction (candidate semantics, not a `RelationKind` token) |
| --- | --- |
| `input` | The exact entity and exact relative object are named. Candidate direct relations include affected-referent, resource-use, parameter-binding, constituent-supply, premise-use, reference-use, operation-argument, transformation-participation, planned-filling, or another exact direct relation. These do not form one input kind or relation family. |
| `raw material` or physical `source material` | The physical entity remains distinct from its exact constituent, affected-referent, consumed-resource, transfer, supply, or transformation relation. The adjective `source` alone does not open `C.2.P`. |
| epistemic `source data` or `source material` | `C.2.P` first recovers the exact source expression, episteme, publication, and current source-to-use relation. The receiving claim then recovers that exact entity's direct relation to the method, plan, work, transformation, evaluation, or receiving use now at issue. |
| `output` | The exact entity and relative object remain explicit. The candidate direct relation is the changed-affected-referent relation, a local entity-identity-inception claim under `A.15.PROD`, or the exact issuance, transfer, delivery, or other subject relation current for the use. A changed continuing entity is not newly constituted merely because it is called an output. |
| `result` | Ordinary `result` may point to heterogeneous governed values or claims; the word supplies no common kind or relation family. The exact referent under its own kind, the exact relative object, and the direct relation or relation-bearing claim make that use true. Illustrative, non-exhaustive cases include an entity whose production or identity-inception history is separately governed; a continuing affected referent whose change history is separately governed; an enduring condition expressed by an exact fact or relation; a `C.11` `ChoiceResult`; a measured characteristic value; a `C.2.1` episteme carrying a measurement, comparison, or evaluation claim; diagnostic-finding claim content or the episteme carrying it; an acceptance or other verdict under its direct governor; and a decision occurrence or decision record. These cases remain separately governed and are not one result kind or family. Plain `result` remains only while the exact referent, relative object, and direct governor stay recoverable. |
| `outcome` | A downstream subject effect is recovered when that is the claim. When the word reports evaluation, the exact characteristic reading, comparison result, or verdict produced by separate evaluation work is recovered instead. Success class, measured value, verdict, and downstream effect do not share one slot. |
| `deliverable` | The entity is recovered separately in each current planning, commitment, delivery, and acceptance relation. A planned deliverable is not already produced; an output need not be deliverable; one accepted deliverable may assemble entities produced by several work occurrences. |
| `handoff` | An actual transfer-work occurrence or direct transfer relation is recovered. A handoff package or record is an episteme; transfer, package, result, delivery, and receiving use remain distinct. `E.10.MOVE` coordinates a process-baton case only when that exact move is current. |

When no direct governor closes the selected row, the result is a blocker naming the exact participant meanings, proposed obtaining question, affected receiving use, and future owner. A broader hypernym, another boundary word, or a new universal record does not settle the missing relation.

#### A.6.P.WMR:4.8 - Work-name grounding by morphology and occurrence

An action nominal such as `testing`, `assembly`, `maintenance`, `evaluation`, or `inspection` is a morphology cue, not an occurrence identification or recovered kind. Placement in function- or flow-structure prose does not identify a `U.Function` or any other object by itself. When the use remains function-like and claim-bearing while its exact FPF object or relation is hidden, `A.6.F` is the next owner. When the object is already recoverable, the label resolves to the exact `U.Method`, `U.MethodDescription`, required-transformation or required-effect claim, actual `U.Transformation`, `TransformationFlowStructure` locus, `FunctionalElement@Context` or other functional-view record, plan content, performed Work occurrence admitted under `U.Work`, or other governed value under its direct pattern. In a WBS element, activity, or Work Package the nominal ordinarily names plan or assignment content about intended work; none of these uses identifies an already performed Work occurrence.

A use that needs only the recovered method, method description, plan, structure, or other already governed value closes under that direct pattern. Only reliance on the label as one performed occurrence routes the candidate designation and required granularity to `A.15.1`.

WMR consumes only the direct A.15.1 return: one exact Work occurrence admitted under `U.Work` at the granularity needed by the receiver, an exact lowering to the neighboring method, description, plan, evidence, telemetry, temporal, or other object actually supported, or an exact blocker. A materially needed `workContinuityPolicyRef` remains part of the A.15.1 identity judgment rather than a WMR field. When one occurrence is established and needs a durable name, `F.18` opens after that result.

Any output, result, outcome, production, delivery, or acceptance wording is a separate WMR claim only while its direct relation or claim dimensions remain hidden. Once readable, it proceeds under its own pattern and does not become part of work identity.

The preceding action-nominal routing is an FPF-scoped synthesis from recurring morphology-and-owner ambiguity, not a rule imported from PMI, PRINCE2, IDEF0, or IDEF3. A domain may conventionally use an action nominal as the durable name of an already grounded method, plan item, functional-view record, or dated work occurrence; that direct identity and governor win over morphology. The synthesis reopens if repeated practice shows that the cue routes a directly grounded value to the wrong owner, or if a trigger case cannot close through `A.6.F`, the direct owner, or `A.15.1` without new ontology.

| Planning or description lineage | Bounded use here | Prohibited inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PMBOK WBS practice | Its deliverable-oriented naming pressure is used only to recognize that WBS elements and Work Packages are often named from expected deliverables. | The named plan element proves no dated occurrence, enacted method, actual participant, produced entity, result, or outcome. |
| PRINCE2 7 public Foundation page | The page is used only as currentness evidence that PeopleCert presents Version 7 and describes seven recurring project-management practices. It does not expose detailed product, product-description, activity, or Work Package distinctions, so those distinctions are not source authority here. | Neither the page nor PRINCE2 terminology supplies occurrence identity, a positive FPF kind inference, or any production, delivery, or acceptance claim. |
| IDEF0 historical recognition lineage (non-authoritative here) | A box label remains source-side function-model wording. A still-hidden FPF claim routes to `A.6.F`; otherwise the label names the already recovered required-transformation or required-effect claim, actual `U.Transformation`, functional-view record, method description, `TransformationFlowStructure` locus, or other exact governed value under its direct pattern. | This lineage supplies no positive FPF kind inference: neither box form nor function wording identifies `U.Function` or supplies identity evidence for a performed Work occurrence admitted under `U.Work`. |
| IDEF3 Units-of-Behavior historical recognition lineage (non-authoritative here) | The terminology remains only as wording that may occur in inherited process descriptions. | This lineage supplies no positive FPF kind or relation inference; a Unit-of-Behavior description or label is never identity evidence for a dated Work occurrence admitted under `U.Work`. |

**Inspection contrast.** `inspection method` names the way of doing. `planned Pump-14 inspection work` is intended-work content. `Pump-14 inspection on 2026-07-15 09:10-09:34` becomes an exact performed occurrence only when A.15.1 returns that result at the required granularity; otherwise retain its lowered object or blocker. If the receiver asks whether the work first constituted an inspection-report episteme, A.15.PROD returns either its local entity-identity-inception claim or the exact branch blocker. A measured or diagnostic result remains with its direct result pattern, and a pass/fail verdict remains a separately governed evaluation-result episteme rather than the inspected entity or a downstream effect.
