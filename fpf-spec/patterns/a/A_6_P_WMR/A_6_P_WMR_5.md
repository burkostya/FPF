### A.6.P.WMR:5 - Archetypal Grounding

**Informative worked examples.** These cases demonstrate the checklist results in ordinary language; they add no RFC duty and do not make an example identifier, relation token, or assumed project settlement part of FPF ontology.

**Minimal worked contrast.** A source says, `the inspection report is the result of inspection`.

- If one exact `A.6.1` application actually returned report episteme `R-17`, identified under `C.2.1`, and the exact result declaration gives the result meaning, `ValueKind = U.Episteme`, binding predicate, cardinality, extent, and binding identity, state only that declaration-local binding. The application id, binding occurrence, report episteme, and any assertion about the binding remain distinct. It does not say that a report episteme was first constituted or that inspection work occurred.
- If the receiving question is whether exact work `W-17` first constituted report `R-17`, hand that question to A.15.PROD and consume either its local entity-identity-inception claim or its exact blocker. Keep the measured value, diagnostic finding, and evaluation verdict separate.
- If neither relation is governed, write the blocker naming `R-17`, the proposed inspection application or work occurrence, the unresolved obtaining question, and the direct owner needed. Do not settle the gap with `WorkResult` or a report-shaped output kind.

The same trigger phrase can therefore close through different direct claims. The exact related object and obtaining relation, not the word `result`, decide the answer.
